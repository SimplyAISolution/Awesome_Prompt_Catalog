## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Choose a prompt that fits your task.
2. Copy the prompt text from the file.
3. Replace any `[bracketed placeholders]` with your specific details.
4. Paste into your AI model of choice and run.

## 💡 Tips for General Prompts

- **Be specific**: The more context you provide, the better the output.
- **Iterate**: Use the follow-up suggestions included in each prompt to refine results.
- **Combine**: Chain multiple prompts together for complex workflows (e.g., Research Helper → Summarizer → Writing Assistant).
