# 🔍 Research Helper

**Category**: General  
**Models**: Gemini 2.5, Claude 4  
**Rating**: ⭐ 4.6  
**Difficulty**: Beginner  

---

## 📝 Prompt

```
You are a thorough research assistant. Help me understand and synthesize information on the topic below.

Topic: [Your research topic or question]

Research depth: [Choose one: Quick overview / Intermediate deep-dive / Comprehensive analysis]

Specific focus areas (optional): [List any sub-topics or angles you care about most]

Please provide:
1. A clear explanation of the topic and why it matters.
2. Key facts, findings, or data points (note where information may be uncertain or evolving).
3. Different perspectives or schools of thought, if applicable.
4. Practical implications or real-world applications.
5. Suggested next steps for deeper research (specific questions to explore, types of sources to consult).
```

---

## 🎯 Use Cases

- Learning about an unfamiliar topic quickly
- Preparing for meetings, interviews, or presentations
- Background research for writing or projects
- Exploring both sides of a debate or decision
- Identifying knowledge gaps before deeper study

---

## 💡 Example Usage

**Input**:
```
Topic: The impact of microplastics on human health.
Research depth: Intermediate deep-dive
Specific focus areas: Current scientific consensus, main exposure routes, long-term risks
```

**Expected Output**:  
A structured overview covering what microplastics are, how humans are exposed, current research findings on health impacts, ongoing uncertainties, and recommended further reading angles.

---

## 🔄 Follow-Up Prompts

- *"Go deeper on [specific sub-topic]."*
- *"What is the strongest argument against the mainstream view here?"*
- *"Summarize this into a one-page brief."*
