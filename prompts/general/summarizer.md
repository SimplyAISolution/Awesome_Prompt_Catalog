# 📄 Summarizer

**Category**: General  
**Models**: All  
**Rating**: ⭐ 4.8  
**Difficulty**: Beginner  

---

## 📝 Prompt

```
Summarize the following content clearly and concisely.

Content to summarize:
"""
[Paste your text, article, document, or notes here]
"""

Requirements:
- Length: [One sentence / One paragraph / Bullet points / ~[N] words]
- Audience: [General public / Technical expert / Executive / Student]
- Focus: [Key findings / Action items / Main arguments / All main points]

Provide the summary, then list up to 3 key takeaways as bullet points.
```

---

## 🎯 Use Cases

- Summarizing long articles or research papers
- Condensing meeting notes or transcripts
- Creating executive summaries for reports
- Digesting documentation or technical content
- Summarizing email threads

---

## 💡 Example Usage

**Input**:
```
Content to summarize:
"""
[Paste a 1,000-word news article about renewable energy adoption trends]
"""

Requirements:
- Length: One paragraph
- Audience: General public
- Focus: Key findings
```

**Expected Output**:  
A single paragraph summarizing the article's main points, followed by 3 bullet-point takeaways.

---

## 🔄 Follow-Up Prompts

- *"Make the summary shorter — just 2 sentences."*
- *"Rewrite the summary for a technical audience."*
- *"What are the most important statistics mentioned?"*
