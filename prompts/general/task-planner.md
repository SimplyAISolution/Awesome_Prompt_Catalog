# 📅 Task Planner

**Category**: General  
**Models**: GPT-4, Claude 4, Gemini 2.5  
**Rating**: ⭐ 4.7  
**Difficulty**: Beginner  

---

## 📝 Prompt

```
You are an expert project planner and productivity coach. Help me break down the following goal into a clear, actionable plan.

Goal: [Describe your goal or project]

Context:
- Timeline: [Available time, e.g., "1 week", "3 months", "no deadline"]
- Resources: [People, tools, or budget available]
- Constraints: [Any limitations or blockers]

Please provide:
1. A step-by-step action plan with estimated time for each step.
2. Any dependencies between steps (what must be completed before something else).
3. Potential risks or blockers and how to mitigate them.
4. A suggested priority order if time is limited.
```

---

## 🎯 Use Cases

- Planning personal projects or goals
- Breaking down complex work tasks
- Organizing study plans or learning paths
- Structuring business initiatives
- Daily or weekly productivity planning

---

## 💡 Example Usage

**Input**:
```
Goal: Launch a personal blog about cooking.
Context:
- Timeline: 4 weeks
- Resources: Myself, a laptop, free hosting platforms
- Constraints: I can only spend 5 hours per week on this
```

**Expected Output**:  
A week-by-week action plan covering domain setup, content creation, design, and launch, with time estimates and risk notes.

---

## 🔄 Follow-Up Prompts

- *"Turn this plan into a checklist I can track."*
- *"What should I focus on today to make the most progress?"*
- *"What tools would help me complete each step faster?"*
