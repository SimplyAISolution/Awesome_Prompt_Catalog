# ✍️ Writing Assistant

**Category**: General  
**Models**: All  
**Rating**: ⭐ 4.7  
**Difficulty**: Beginner  

---

## 📝 Prompt

```
You are a professional writing coach and editor. Help me with the following writing task.

Task type: [Choose one: Draft / Edit & Improve / Proofread / Rewrite / Expand / Shorten]

My text (leave blank if drafting from scratch):
"""
[Paste your existing text here, or leave empty]
"""

Details:
- Topic or purpose: [What is this text for?]
- Tone: [Formal / Conversational / Persuasive / Informative / Friendly]
- Audience: [Who will read this?]
- Length target: [Word count or description, e.g., "~200 words" or "one paragraph"]

Deliver the final text, then briefly explain the main changes or choices you made.
```

---

## 🎯 Use Cases

- Drafting emails, messages, or letters
- Improving blog posts, essays, or articles
- Proofreading for grammar and clarity
- Rewriting content for a different audience or tone
- Expanding bullet points into full paragraphs
- Shortening verbose text

---

## 💡 Example Usage

**Input**:
```
Task type: Edit & Improve

My text:
"""
Our product is really good and people like it a lot. We have many customers who buy from us every month and they are happy. You should try it.
"""

Details:
- Topic or purpose: Product description for a website homepage
- Tone: Persuasive and professional
- Audience: Potential business customers
- Length target: ~50 words
```

**Expected Output**:  
A polished, professional rewrite of the text plus a short explanation of improvements made.

---

## 🔄 Follow-Up Prompts

- *"Make it more formal."*
- *"Add a strong call-to-action at the end."*
- *"Rewrite this for a younger, more casual audience."*
